More Than Rice

A commitment to quality, in every grain we mill.

Param Rice is a Bangalore-headquartered rice business operating its own rice mill, working with a wide range of paddy and rice varieties. Every sack that leaves our mill carries the same intent: consistency you can rely on, batch after batch.

We work closely with the entire journey — from sourcing paddy to cleaning, processing, grading and packaging — so that what reaches your kitchen or your business is rice we'd trust on our own table.
